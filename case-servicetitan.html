Case Study SaaS Website Redesign

How ServiceTitan
could convert more
with a cleaner UX

A modern redesign concept focused on improving user experience, simplifying navigation, and creating a clearer path to product discovery and demo booking.

Client ServiceTitan
Role UI/UX Design · Website Redesign
Scope Concept Project
Year 2024

The Problem

Complex navigation creates friction for first-time visitors

ServiceTitan offers a powerful suite of software solutions for home and commercial service businesses. However, the large volume of products, resources, and navigation options can make it difficult for new visitors to quickly understand the platform and identify the right solution for their needs.

Before → Current Site
After → Redesign Concept

Approach

Simplified user journey

The redesign restructures the information architecture to help visitors move from discovery to demo request with fewer distractions and clearer decision points. Key improvements include a streamlined navigation structure, an interactive product showcase, and a refined user flow that reduces cognitive load at every step.
